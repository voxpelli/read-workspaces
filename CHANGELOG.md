* Node engine range bumped from `^22.13.0 || >=24.0.0` to `^22.22.2 || ^24.15.0 || >=26.0.0` to align with npm 12. All three runtime dependencies were bumped to new major versions.
* drops support for Node.js 18 and 20; minimum is now Node.js 22.13.0.
